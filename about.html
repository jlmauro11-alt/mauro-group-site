Inbound guidelines

If you reach out, include enough context to evaluate quickly.

Include

  • What you’re building and why now
  • Team background and technical differentiation
  • Traction, metrics, and timeline
  • What you want (capital, introductions, operating help)

Avoid

  • Overly long decks without an executive summary
  • Unclear business model or vague problem definition
  • Hype-driven timelines with no evidence
  • Mass outreach with no fit
